The Helidon team is pleased to announce Helidon 27, our first release under the Tip-and-Tail model used by OpenJDK. This release changes how Helidon moves with Java. Teams that want the latest JDK and Helidon features can follow the Tip, while teams that need a longer-lived production baseline can remain on the Tail.
